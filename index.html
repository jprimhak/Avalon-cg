Rules & temporary assumptions

Choose two different cards per exchange and select Attack or Defence for each. Both players reveal together. Used cards are unavailable until the end of three exchanges, when all six refresh. Attack and defence values are pooled; damage = max(0, incoming attack − defence). Healing is capped at 10 HP.

C4 attacks for 6 instead of 1 when your stance is Blue, or the AI's stance is Yellow. Stances are independent. Movement shifts one position left or right; choose direction on your movement cards. Defence resolves first in initiative order, then attack resolves in initiative order (which may have changed during defence). Damage is pooled and applied after effects. The exact timing, initiative tie-break and AI strategy are provisional and can be changed.
