Rules & temporary assumptions

Choose two different cards per exchange: one Attack and one Defence. Both players reveal together. Used cards are unavailable until the end of three exchanges, when all six refresh. Attack and defence values are pooled; damage = max(0, incoming attack − defence). Stance-changing effects let you choose Yellow, Green or Blue.

C4 attacks for 6 instead of 1 when your stance is Blue, or the AI's stance is Yellow. Stances are independent. Stance changes select a colour directly. Both Defence cards are revealed first and resolved in initiative order. Tap to reveal and resolve both Attack cards, then tap to dismiss. Initiative changes during Defence affect Attack order. Damage is pooled and applied after effects. The exact timing, initiative tie-break and AI strategy are provisional and can be changed.
