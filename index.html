Prototype rules / assumptions

You start at track position 2, Hound at 6, on a seven-position track. Your stance starts at 1 (range 1–4). Attacks only deal damage if adjacent. Non-attack effects still work out of range. Movement is clamped at track edges and cannot pass through the opponent; you can choose movement direction. Stance is clamped 1–4. Enemy movement approaches you; push moves the enemy away. Defence reduces incoming attacks for the current turn, without being consumed. Effects resolve immediately, top phase then bottom phase, in initiative order; initiative changes affect subsequent actions. Hound turns are drawn randomly with replacement. Player cards refresh after four turns. Conditional effects are evaluated when executed. These assumptions can be refined.
